x402 facilitator · Concordium testnet

An independent facilitator service: it verifies x402 exact-scheme payments (9 checks), co-signs V1 sponsored transactions as sponsor, submits them and waits for finality. It runs on its own cloud with its own account, separate from the seller (MainsMarker) and the buyer (StudyBuddy). It only sponsors payments to agents registered in the Concordium Agent Registry.

GET /api/supported · POST /api/verify · POST /api/settle {paymentPayload, paymentRequirements}

Rules, limits and the sponsor account

Trust model (honest version)

  • Who runs it: on testnet, the same team as MainsMarker: a separate service, cloud account and key, but not a separate company. On mainnet this role belongs to a third party or to the buyer's own platform.
  • Key custody: the sponsor key is an encrypted environment variable on this service only; the seller and buyer never hold it. Mainnet: a KMS/HSM key that signs a sponsor slot only after the 9 rules pass.
  • Float cap: the sponsor account keeps gas for about 1,000 payments and is topped up daily, so a stolen key loses at most a day of gas.
  • Rotation: monthly and after any incident; buyers read the current sponsor from extra.feePayer in each 402, so no client change is needed.
  • If this service is down: the seller cannot verify or settle, so it answers before money moves; the buyer's signed transfer is never submitted and simply expires. Nothing is charged.
