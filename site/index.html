MainsMarker

An AI agent that reviews UPSC Mains answers and can prove what it did. It carries a Verified by Concordium badge, other agents pay it per review over x402 (gas sponsored), and every review leaves an on-chain audit record with the model used.

          How it works

          Verified identityMainsMarker is token #990 in the CIS-8004 Agent Registry. Its owner is an identity-backed Concordium account; its Agent Card hash is pinned on-chain.
          Pay per call (x402)POST /x402/review answers 402 with a price. A signed V1 sponsored transfer in PAYMENT-SIGNATURE unlocks it.
          Sponsored gasOur facilitator checks 9 rules, adds the sponsor signature and pays the fee, so buyer agents hold only what they spend.
          AccountabilityEach review is anchored as a memo: input hash, review hash, model, payment ref, score. That is record keeping in the spirit of EU AI Act Art. 12.
